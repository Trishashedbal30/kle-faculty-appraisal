# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors are expected to communicate respectfully, give constructive feedback, and make participation welcoming regardless of background or experience. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and personal attacks are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies to project discussions, issue and pull request activity, and other spaces where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Contact the project maintainers through a private channel if one has been established. No contact address is currently listed; do not include sensitive or personal information in public reports. Maintainers may edit or remove unacceptable contributions and may restrict participation when necessary.
